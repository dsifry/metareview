## What landed

- **`internal/fsm/run/proof.go` (new):** `DifferentialProof{ID,Finding,Kind,Test,Pin,Deletes}`,
  `DeletionRef`, `ProofResult` (= `PinResult` with a `DifferentialProof` in place of `Pin`, §9.1),
  the `Proof{Reproduction,Pin,Deletion}` kind constants, `Valid()` (the one-of invariant), and a
  custom `UnmarshalJSON` that rejects a malformed record **at decode** (unknown field + one-of
  violation), per §9.1 "enforced at decode".
- **`internal/fsm/run/types.go`:** `Delta.Pins`/`Delta.PinResults` keep their §2.4 names but carry the
  §9.1 generalization (`[]DifferentialProof`/`[]ProofResult`), so a reproduction/deletion proof rides
  the same carrier and **no proof kind is omitted**. `Snapshot.Unproven` generalized to
  `[]DifferentialProof`. `PinMalformed` doc **widened** to cover a compiles-but-semantically-null
  mutation (§2.2/§9.8 R7). The superseded `run.PinResult` struct removed (its role is `ProofResult`;
  the mutate-a-line engine keeps its own pin-only result in `internal/mutation`). `Clone` deep-copies
  proofs including the `Pin`/`Deletes` pointer targets (`cloneProofs`).
- **`internal/fsm/run/fold.go`:** `withinCaps` now caps the persisted `Delta.Pins`/`PinResults` at
  `MaxDeltaList`, with per-field caps on every proof/pin/deletion/result field (`proofWithinCaps`).
- **`internal/mutation/verify.go`:** `PinMalformed` doc widened to match §2.2; no behaviour change
  (the AST pre-screen that reclassifies a trivial pin is T1.6).

### Design decision (reconciling spec + plan)
`Delta.Pins`/`PinResults` **retain their field names** (spec §2.4 keeps them: "§9.1 GENERALIZES
these") while their **element types generalize** to `DifferentialProof`/`ProofResult` (§9.1: "the
tagged wire contract"). This is the single wire carrier for all three proof kinds. The old WIP
`Delta.Pins []Pin` never shipped, so there is no persisted run in the `[]Pin` shape to migrate.
`SchemaVersion` stays **1**, additive-optional — the existing runs still `Fold`. The `Unproven`
fold **lifecycle** (temporal add/clear/re-add by `Finding`) is deferred to T1.3 per the plan.
